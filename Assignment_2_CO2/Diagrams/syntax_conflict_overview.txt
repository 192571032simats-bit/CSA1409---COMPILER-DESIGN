Grammar conflict overview

LL(1) conflict:
S -> aS | a
Conflict at M[S, a]

Parse tree conflict:
E -> E + E | E - E | id
Ambiguous input: id + id - id

Dangling else:
S -> if E then S | if E then S else S | a
Conflict resolved by nearest-then rule.

Operator precedence:
E -> E + E | E * E | id
Conflict resolved by splitting precedence levels.
